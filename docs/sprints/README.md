# Sprints

LoopLab runs two sprints, each tracked as a GitHub milestone on the [project board](https://github.com/users/kishankumar5/projects/1).

| Sprint | Dates | Milestone | Doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Mon Nov 9 to Tue Nov 10, 2026 | [Sprint 1](https://github.com/kishankumar5/looplab/milestone/1) | [sprint-1.md](sprint-1.md) |
| Sprint 2 | Wed Nov 11 to Thu Nov 12, 2026 | [Sprint 2](https://github.com/kishankumar5/looplab/milestone/2) | [sprint-2.md](sprint-2.md) |

**Why two-day sprints:** our original plan (Sprint 1 ending Nov 4) slipped while we finished HW3, so on Nov 9 we re-planned the remaining work into two short sprints that still end on the Nov 12 deadline.

**Standups:** each of us posts a written standup twice a day (morning and evening) in the sprint doc: what I did, what's next, what's blocking me. That gives 3+ per sprint per person.

**Estimates** are story points (1 = under an hour with AI help, 2 = a couple of hours, 3 = half a day, 5 = most of a day, 8 = too big, split it).
